HOW TO PLAY
EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

CONTROLS

Keyboard: ARROWS or WASD move · SPACE attack · SHIFT special · R super · P (or CTRL) parry · ENTER (or Z for the one before) next weapon · Q / E abilities (F, G, V, B for extra slots) · 1-4 use an item · M sound.

Touch: the pad on the left moves you; the buttons on the right attack, use your special, super, parry and fire your abilities; the small < and > buttons step back and forward through your weapons (or tap a weapon in the rack). Tap an item in the bar to use it.

Click or tap a weapon in the rack at the top to switch straight to it. Hold attack, special, super or an ability and it fires again by itself whenever it is ready.

MODES

PvP – two players fight; each round you have 3 lives, and the first to win 3 rounds takes the match.

Create a room / Join a room – make your own PvP room with a name, 2 to 4 players and a map size, and wait for others to find it in the room list; or browse the open rooms (name, host, who is in it, how many players, map size) and jump in. With 3 or 4 players it is last one standing: the round goes to whoever is still alive when everyone else is out.

Map size – pick SMALL, MEDIUM or BIG on the title screen before any mode; a bigger map gives more room to move (and zooms the view out). Quick PvP and co-op only pair you with someone who picked the same size.

Quick chat – press T or tap CHAT in a match for a list of friendly lines (hello, good luck, GG...); it shows as a bubble over your head. Only these lines can be sent.

Bot Battle – the same PvP match, against a bot. Pick its level: Easy, Common, Average, Strong, Hard or Insane. The higher the level, the more it owns: every weapon, ability and skin has a 10% chance of being the bot's on Easy, up to 60% on Insane, and its weapons are upgraded that far too and the faster and sharper it plays: dodging shots, parrying, and using specials, supers, abilities and items. It fights with random weapons from what it owns and swaps every so often. Bots collect floor powerups like you do (4 at most) and only use what they picked up. The Admin bot is the final test: every weapon fully maxed (all passives live), only the best abilities, and it leans on the legendaries.

Co-op – two players fight 10 waves of monsters together; the Giant waits at wave 10.

Waves – solo and endless; every wave is harder, a Giant comes every 20th wave, and your best wave goes on the leaderboard.

Extreme – solo with the toughest monsters, a Giant every 5th wave and much bigger coin rewards.

The Portal Mage – the hardest boss: win 50,000 coins, 50,000 XP and the Portal Wand.

Sandbox – solo practice with every weapon. Spawn any monster, boss, item or trap and switch on cheats from the TOOLS button. Nothing you do there is saved.

WEAPONS & XP

Kills give XP, and XP unlocks new weapons for good. Each weapon has its own attack and a special with a cooldown (the purple bar). Melee weapons hit what is in front of you; ranged ones auto-aim at the nearest enemy. Newer ones: the Scimitar (dash), Slingshot (stones knock foes back), Mace (stuns), Javelin (pierces), Claws (heal you as they cut), Ember Staff (burns), Halberd (huge reach) and Frost Bow (slows).

Legendary weapons are bought with coins in UPGRADE WEAPONS and are the only ones with a SUPER (R). The Fire Glove needs every other weapon first; the Vortex Shield needs nothing; the Wind Wand, Explosive Revolver, Stormbreaker, Winter's Edge, Sunfire Longbow, Chrono Staff (TIME STOP freezes every foe around you, REWIND undoes your wounds) and Void Blade (void waves, RIFT STEP, and a SINGULARITY that collapses in a huge blast) need another legendary; the Mind Tome (lays psychic traps under your enemies, spins beams that freeze them, and its SUPER takes over the strongest enemy on the map – you vanish and fight with its attacks, and SPECIAL uses its own ability (a charger charges, a bomber detonates, a necromancer raises skeletons for you, Light chains curving dashes, and so on); its passive moves every trap next to your enemies every 30s, with a countdown over your head) needs the Storm Tome fully maxed and three other legendaries; the Portal Wand can only be won from the Portal Mage; and the rarest of all, the Dagger of Ghosts, can only be claimed once you own every other weapon in the game. The Light Blade (dash wherever you click or tap and swipe as you land, chain five curving dashes that hurl foes into the walls, and go LIGHTSPEED for triple speed and lightning crashes) is claimed by beating Light 3 times, over any number of runs, with at least 75% of all weapons fully maxed.

PARRY

Parry (P) opens a short window: a melee blow aimed at you is sent back at the attacker, a shot bounces back the way it came, and a fire hand or inferno fizzles out. Parrying a Giant's swing or shockwave stuns him. It then needs 8 seconds to recharge (the blue bar).

COINS & UPGRADES

Monsters drop coins – walk over them to collect. Spend coins in UPGRADE WEAPONS (damage, speed, range, crit, cooldown, multishot and more for each weapon, plus your health, move speed, defense and parry), on ABILITIES, and on skins in CUSTOMIZE.

★ PASSIVES – max out every upgrade on one of these weapons and, whenever you hold it, you get its passive (its name shows under your weapon bar, and you glow in its colour): Dagger SWIFTNESS +45% move speed, with wind streaks behind you · Sword GUARDIAN take 30% less damage · Axe BERSERKER up to +100% damage the lower your health · Bow EAGLE EYE +30% range, +18% crit · Staff ARCANE FLOW special recharges 50% faster · Katana BLADE DANCE 30% faster attacks · Reaper SOUL HARVEST every kill heals 12% · Frost Rod FROST AURA heavily slows monsters near you · Fire Glove EMBER SKIN you never burn, and attackers get scorched and catch fire · Storm Tome STATIC CHARGE zaps the nearest foe every 1.2s.
Every legendary has one too: Fire Glove EMBER SKIN (above) · Vortex Shield AEGIS blocks a whole hit every 4s · Wind Wand GALE GUARD blows away enemy shots that get close (every 0.5s) · Explosive Revolver CHAIN REACTION every hit explodes onto nearby foes and every kill blows up even bigger · Portal Wand ESCAPE PORTAL warps you to safety and heals 20% when a hit drops you below 35% · Dagger of Ghosts HAUNTING your thrown daggers chase down your enemies · Stormbreaker THUNDERSTRUCK 35% of your hits call down lightning for +80% · Winter's Edge SHATTERPOINT +50% damage to slowed or frozen foes · Sunfire Longbow SUNLIT heal 5% every second · Light Blade AFTERGLOW every dash leaves a streak of light that burns and stuns. The upgrade menu shows each one and how close you are. Try them all in Sandbox with ALL PASSIVES.

ABILITIES

Bought once and kept forever. Put them in your slots in the ABILITIES menu: you start with two (Q and E) and can buy up to four more (F, G, V, B) for huge sums. There are 40 to collect: movement (dash, shadow step), healing and protection (second wind, rejuvenate, aegis, barrier, vanish, phoenix), damage (meteor shower, thunderstorm, chain lightning, fire nova, airstrike, ice lance, execute and more), control (frost nova, time warp, black hole, gravity grip, force push, earthquake) and summons (call to arms, stone golem, fire sentry). Newest: Perfect Guard (a 2.5s parry), Safe Haven (warp to safety and heal), Blade Flurry, Landmines, Soul Chain (drag a foe to you), War Cry, Overcharge (special and super ready again), Fireball, Smite and Frost Armor. Each has its own cooldown.

ITEMS

Glowing gems appear on the floor. Walk over one to pick it up (you can carry 4), then use it with 1-4 or by tapping it: speed, strength, shield, haste, heal, regeneration, vampire, bomb, frost nova, thorns (attackers take half back), cloak (invisible), lightning jar (zaps 5 foes), turret, monster egg (2 monsters fight for you), frenzy (fast, strong and quick), iron skin (half damage), hourglass (slows every foe), gold rush (double coins) and elixir (full heal and cure).

TRAPS

Traps appear around the arena and hurt everyone, monsters included – so lure enemies onto them. Spikes, fire vents and tesla coils glow while they arm, then go off. Others: mines, snares, poison pools, spring pads and warp runes. Watch out too for frost plates (freeze), saw pits, gravity wells (drag you in), lava cracks, root vines (can't move), powder kegs (huge blast), mirror runes (controls reversed), null fields (no specials, supers or abilities) and fault lines – and the kind one, the healing spring.

MONSTERS & BOSSES

Each kind fights its own way: runners are fast, brutes hit hard, spitters and infernals shoot from range, wraiths blink next to you, and armoured ones (grey mark on their health bar) shrug off part of every hit.

Bombers light a fuse next to you and blow up (get out of the ring, or parry) – kill one first and it blows up its own pack. Shamans hang back healing and warding the monsters around them (green glow: they take less damage), so hunt them first. Splitters burst into two quick slimelets. Chargers lower their heads and show a red line, then ram along it – step aside and they hit the wall dazed, or parry them. Necromancers raise skeletons from green marks, and every skeleton crumbles when the necromancer dies.

Light is rare (from wave 6, never two waves running) and usually comes alone. He is blindingly fast: a glinting arc means a lunging blade sweep, after which he blinks somewhere else; a dashed arrow means a chain of four dashes that curve after you. Parry either to stop him and leave him dazed. Beat him three times to earn your way toward the Light Blade.

Reading what hit a monster: ice = a block of ice with icicles · stun = gold stars circling its head · shock = blue lightning arcs · quake = rubble and dust at its feet · time stop = a gold bubble with a stopped clock · roots = green vines · chained = iron chains and a padlock · smite = a halo and a pillar of light · rift = a swirling purple vortex · silenced = a struck-through spark. Slowed monsters are tinted too: cyan with snowflakes (frost), amber with an hourglass (time), rope-brown (snared) or green with bubbles (toxic cloud).

When you die you come back at the safest spot in the arena, away from monsters, traps and shots.

The Giant swings his tree and slams the ground to send out a shockwave – parry either one to stun him. The Portal Mage throws fireballs through red portals, teleports (follow him through the portal if you are quick), lays traps, summons monsters through green portals and, as a last resort, hides behind two Giants – kill them to finish him.

SAVING

Type a password on the start screen and your XP, coins, weapons, upgrades, abilities and skins are saved to it. Use the same password on any device to carry on. A copy is also kept in your browser, so progress survives server updates.
