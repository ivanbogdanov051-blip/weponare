HOW TO PLAY
EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

CONTROLS

Keyboard: ARROWS or WASD move · SPACE attack · SHIFT special · R super · P (or CTRL) parry · ENTER (or Z for the one before) next weapon · Q / E abilities (F, G, V, B for extra slots) · X shield · 1-4 use an item · M sound.

Touch: the pad on the left moves you; the buttons on the right attack, use your special, super, parry and fire your abilities; the small < and > buttons step back and forward through your weapons (or tap a weapon in the rack). Tap an item in the bar to use it.

Click or tap a weapon in the rack at the top to switch straight to it. Hold attack, special, super or an ability and it fires again by itself whenever it is ready.

MODES

PvP – two players fight; each round you have 3 lives, and the first to win 3 rounds takes the match.

Create a room / Join a room – make your own PvP room with a name, 2 to 4 players and a map size, and wait for others to find it in the room list; or browse the open rooms (name, host, who is in it, how many players, map size) and jump in. With 3 or 4 players it is last one standing: the round goes to whoever is still alive when everyone else is out.

Map size – pick SMALL, MEDIUM, BIG or GIANT in Settings before any mode; a bigger map gives more room to move and zooms the view out, so you always see all of it. DUEL is a long strip for 2 players. Quick PvP and co-op only pair you with someone who picked the same size.

Rare monsters – off until you turn them on in Settings. Now and then a monster shines gold: 4x health, 2x speed, harder hits and an extra ability, but it drops a huge pile of coins. In co-op, the player who opens the room decides.

Picking a weapon – every match opens with a 5 second countdown to choose your weapon. Press READY to start sooner. You keep it when you die and come back, and in each new round. You can still swap during a fight.

Quick chat – press T or tap CHAT in a match for a list of friendly lines (hello, good luck, GG...); it shows as a bubble over your head. Only these lines can be sent.

Bot Battle – the same PvP match, against a bot. Pick its level: Easy, Common, Average, Strong, Hard or Insane. The higher the level, the more it owns: every weapon, ability and skin has a 20% chance of being the bot's on Easy, up to 80% on Insane, and its weapons are upgraded that far too and the faster and sharper it plays: dodging shots, parrying, and using specials, supers, abilities and items. It fights with random weapons from what it owns and swaps every so often. Bots collect floor powerups like you do (4 at most) and only use what they picked up. The Admin bot is the final test: every weapon fully maxed (all passives live), only the best abilities, and it leans on the legendaries.

Co-op – two players fight 10 waves of monsters together; the Giant waits at wave 10.

Waves – solo and endless; every wave is harder, a Giant comes every 20th wave, and your best wave goes on the leaderboard.

Extreme – solo with the toughest monsters, a Giant every 5th wave and much bigger coin rewards.

The Portal Mage – the hardest boss: win 50,000 coins, 50,000 XP and the Portal Wand.

Tsunami – a ghost of living water. He swells up and rushes you, sends swift hunting waves, and throws a huge ball of water that bursts into a wave ring. From half health he attacks far more often and gains three more: big splitting waves, a bouncing ball that leaves water behind it, and a cyclone that grabs you and carries you around the arena. Water slows you and drains your health until you get out. Beat him and the mythic Wavestorm can be earned. He hits very hard, but beating him pays 200,000 coins.

The Ice Knight – a knight of ice with a greatsword, a bow and a shield. He costs 2,000,000 coins a fight, win or lose (tap his button twice to pay). Everything he does slows you. He swipes after 2 seconds of warning; shoots an arrow into the sky that lands on a mark 2 seconds later, freezing you and leaving a spike of solid ice; raises his shield (hit him then and it comes back at you, leave him be and he is stunned for 3 seconds and takes double damage); and throws a flying cut you can parry. Under half health he attacks 3 times as often, sends lines of ice spikes chasing you for 2 seconds, and fires ten seeking arrows at once. Under a quarter the whole arena freezes (you slide) and he calls ice minions. Fire is his weakness: fire weapons hit him twice as hard and he burns four times as fast. Beat him and his gear is yours: the ICE KNIGHT armour (the best there is), the FROSTWALL shield, the ICE GREATSWORD and the ICE BOW.

Saw Room – the hardest thing in the game. No monsters, just moving traps, shots from every wall, walls of saws with one gap to slip through and hunters that chase you, all getting worse by the second. You earn 200,000 coins for every full minute you survive. Armour does nothing in here.

Sandbox – solo practice with every weapon. Spawn any monster, boss, item or trap and switch on cheats from the TOOLS button. Nothing you do there is saved.

THE FORGE

Pick a weapon you own and put coins in the fire (500 to 500,000). More coins give a better chance of an upgrade and a much better chance of a rare one; if the metal cracks the coins are gone. Each weapon holds 3 upgrades. Melee weapons have over 30 possible upgrades and ranged weapons over 20. These are passives that work while the weapon is in your hand. The 5 rare upgrades each give an extra ability on C, and a weapon can only hold one. Reset a weapon in the forge to free its slots; the coins do not come back. A weapon with a rare upgrade glows red. Mythics have a forge list of their own: 32 stronger upgrades and 5 rare abilities that no other weapon can get.

ITEMS

In the ARMOURY, under ITEMS: 24 items, bought once and kept for ever, each upgradable to level 3. PASSIVE ones are always on (more health, speed, damage, lifesteal...). ACTIVE ones you use with 5, 6 and 7 (or their buttons on a phone) and then wait for. ONE-TIME-USE ones work once a match. You start with one item slot; buy up to three in all (500,000 coins each) and pick what to take into a fight. Everyone's items show above their name: tap or click one to see what it does. (Items stay home in minigames and the Saw Room.)

CUSTOM WEAPONS

In the 🛒 tab, CUSTOM WEAPON: make your own. Give it a name and a picture (generate one, or use your own image), then pick its moves: ATK, SPECIAL, SUPER and the ULTIMATE (key U or the 🌟 button). Add up to 2 PASSIVES (REGEN, RAGE, DODGE, THORNS, REVIVE). Each move is borrowed from a real weapon and tuned, or BUILT by you: patterns (VOLLEY, RING, RAIN, ORBIT, NOVA, BEAM, DASH, BACKFIRE, MINES, SPIRAL), up to 3 of them one after another as a COMBO (like a DASH, then shots out behind you), up to 4 effects (burn, freeze, chain, explode, split, crit, execute, curse, pull, teleport...) and shots with a picture you draw. Then it is checked: if its POWER is over the limit it cannot be made, so turn something down and try again. It costs ◆ 1,000,000 and ALL your progress (you keep your name and friends). It can never be traded, bet or lost.

TEAM ROOMS AND CONDITIONS

CREATE ROOM can be 1v1, 2v1, 3v1 or 2v2. Once everyone is in, the owner (👑) puts each player on team A or B and presses START. Teammates can't hurt each other and share every coin they pick up. The first team to win 3 rounds takes the match.

CONDITIONS (optional): how much each player of a team pays if their team loses (split between the winners; e.g. you alone against three: you pay 10,000 if you lose, they pay 1,000 each if they do), and WEAPONS AT STAKE: everyone puts up a weapon and the winners take the losers'. Everyone has to ACCEPT first, you need a password, and leaving a match part-way loses it. After each match the room goes back to its lobby to agree the next one.

FREE CHAT (if the room allows it): type your own lines in the chat. Anything nasty can be reported: ten different players reporting someone costs them 1,000,000 coins (or all they have).

SUGGESTION BOX

On the 🎨 tab. Got an idea for the game? With 10,000 coins you can post it (the coins are not spent; up to 3 a day). Everyone can read every idea and like the ones they want most. Ideas the game's maker picks are marked PLANNED, then ADDED when they are in.

FREE FOR ALL

One giant public room for everyone (up to 12), on the PvP tab and in CREATE ROOM. It starts as soon as two people are in, and from then on anyone can jump in at any time. Nobody is ever out: when you die you come back 30 seconds later, somewhere safe. Every kill counts on the scoreboard. If you leave, you can't join again for 50 seconds.

FRIENDS, TRADE AND CHALLENGE

FRIENDS, TRADE and CHALLENGE all work by name: type the name your friend plays under and, if they have the game open, they get a message and answer YES, NO or DON'T NOTIFY TODAY (then you can't ask them anything else until their midnight). YES to a friend request puts you on each other's list, where you see who is online. YES to a trade opens it for both of you. YES to a challenge lets the one who asked pick the map, and you are both sent into a private PvP room. You need a password for all of it.

TRADE

Ask a friend by name (above), or: one player presses START A TRADE and tells the other the code. Each chooses what to give: weapons, coins, abilities or skins. Both have to give something and both have to agree, and any change to an offer clears both agreements. A weapon keeps its upgrades and forge work when it changes hands, and you can receive a weapon you do not have the XP for. What you give is gone, but your other weapons stay yours. To get a lost weapon back, earn the XP between it and the weapon before it. Legendaries take 2,500 XP, then you buy or win them again. Mythics and the two starter weapons can not be traded.

WEAPONS & XP

Finish the tutorial and the password you play with gets a random common weapon and 2,000 coins (once per password). New commons: the Trident (long thrusts that shove, a piercing throw), Nunchaku (very fast spinning strikes) and Musket (one slow, very heavy shot).

UNCOMMON weapons are better than commons and have a special but no super: the Gladius, War Pike, Morning Star, Twin Blades, Longbow, Hand Cannon, Thunder Rod, Frost Fang, Cinder Bow and Saw Disc. They are never sold: each MINIGAME you win gives you one you don't have yet, at random.

Kills give XP, and XP unlocks new weapons for good. Each weapon has its own attack and a special with a cooldown (the purple bar). Melee weapons hit what is in front of you; ranged ones auto-aim at the nearest enemy. Newer ones: the Scimitar (dash), Slingshot (stones knock foes back), Mace (stuns), Javelin (pierces), Claws (heal you as they cut), Ember Staff (burns), Halberd (huge reach) and Frost Bow (slows).

Legendary weapons are bought with coins in the SHOP and are the only ones with a SUPER (R). The Fire Glove needs every other weapon first; the Vortex Shield needs nothing; the Wind Wand, Explosive Revolver, Stormbreaker, Winter's Edge, Sunfire Longbow, Chrono Staff (TIME STOP freezes every foe around you, REWIND undoes your wounds) and Void Blade (void waves, RIFT STEP, and a SINGULARITY that collapses in a huge blast) need another legendary; the Mind Tome (lays psychic traps under your enemies, spins beams that freeze them, and its SUPER takes over the strongest enemy on the map – you vanish and fight with its attacks, and SPECIAL uses its own ability (a charger charges, a bomber detonates, a necromancer raises skeletons for you, Light chains curving dashes, and so on); its passive moves every trap next to your enemies every 30s, with a countdown over your head) needs the Storm Tome fully maxed and three other legendaries; the Portal Wand can only be won from the Portal Mage; and the rarest of all, the Dagger of Ghosts, can only be claimed once you own every other weapon in the game. The Light Blade (dash wherever you click or tap and swipe as you land, chain five curving dashes that hurl foes into the walls, and go LIGHTSPEED for triple speed and lightning crashes) is claimed by beating Light 3 times, over any number of runs, with at least 75% of all weapons fully maxed.

PARRY

Parry (P) opens a short window: a melee blow aimed at you is sent back at the attacker, a shot bounces back the way it came, and a fire hand or inferno fizzles out. Parrying a Giant's swing or shockwave stuns him. It then needs 8 seconds to recharge (the blue bar).

COINS & UPGRADES

The Armoury sells armour, with new stock every 10 minutes. There are 120 pieces in six rarities, from Common (a little health) to Mythic (huge health and 3 or 4 passives, and they glow red). You can own as many as you like and wear one helmet, one chestplate and one pair of leggings. What you buy stays yours, and armour can be traded. It does nothing in the Saw Room.

Events are hosted for a while (each says when it ends) and can be hosted again later. They have their own fights and prizes, some found nowhere else. The first is the Void Dungeon: pay 200,000 coins to enter a giant void arena and face the rare Abyss (four times the health, bigger, faster, far harder hits, bigger attacks and a new one, ECLIPSE: a ring of void marks closes round you, then one under your feet). Beat him and touch the trophy he leaves to win the mythic shield VOID GATE, which only comes from this event, and 1,000,000 coins plus your 200,000 back. Lose, or leave, and the 200,000 is gone. The second is the Secret Bot: a bot as strong as the INSANE one with the secret FIRE SPEAR, which you win by beating it. Once you beat an event it leaves your list, until it is hosted again.

Shields are sold there too, and go in a slot of their own. There are 56, in the same six rarities. A shield gives no health. Press the shield button (X on a keyboard) and for 5 seconds a small forcefield takes hits in place of your health, until it has taken all it can and breaks; any hit that breaks it carries on through to you. It is ready again 10 seconds after you pressed it. Rarer shields take more, and pressing the button sets off up to 4 abilities: take 50% less damage for 5 seconds, teleport the way you were moving, burn or freeze whoever is close, heal and more. Some have a passive as well.

Monsters drop coins – walk over them to collect. Spend coins in UPGRADE WEAPONS (damage, speed, range, crit, cooldown, multishot and more for each weapon, plus your health, move speed, defense and parry), on ABILITIES, and in CUSTOMIZE on skins, changing colors, glows, animated accessories and nickname backgrounds. Tap one to try it on before you buy it.

★ PASSIVES – max out every upgrade on one of these weapons and, whenever you hold it, you get its passive (its name shows under your weapon bar, and you glow in its colour): Dagger SWIFTNESS +45% move speed, with wind streaks behind you · Sword GUARDIAN take 30% less damage · Axe BERSERKER up to +100% damage the lower your health · Bow EAGLE EYE +30% range, +18% crit · Staff ARCANE FLOW special recharges 50% faster · Katana BLADE DANCE 30% faster attacks · Reaper SOUL HARVEST every kill heals 12% · Frost Rod FROST AURA heavily slows monsters near you · Fire Glove EMBER SKIN you never burn, and attackers get scorched and catch fire · Storm Tome STATIC CHARGE zaps the nearest foe every 1.2s.
Every legendary has one too: Fire Glove EMBER SKIN (above) · Vortex Shield AEGIS blocks a whole hit every 4s · Wind Wand GALE GUARD blows away enemy shots that get close (every 0.5s) · Explosive Revolver CHAIN REACTION every hit explodes onto nearby foes and every kill blows up even bigger · Portal Wand ESCAPE PORTAL warps you to safety and heals 20% when a hit drops you below 35% · Dagger of Ghosts HAUNTING your thrown daggers chase down your enemies · Stormbreaker THUNDERSTRUCK 35% of your hits call down lightning for +80% · Winter's Edge SHATTERPOINT +50% damage to slowed or frozen foes · Sunfire Longbow SUNLIT heal 5% every second · Light Blade AFTERGLOW every dash leaves a streak of light that burns and stuns. The upgrade menu shows each one and how close you are. Try them all in Sandbox with ALL PASSIVES.

ABILITIES

Bought once and kept forever. Put them in your slots in the ABILITIES menu: you start with two (Q and E) and can buy up to four more (F, G, V, B) for huge sums. There are 40 to collect: movement (dash, shadow step), healing and protection (second wind, rejuvenate, aegis, barrier, vanish, phoenix), damage (meteor shower, thunderstorm, chain lightning, fire nova, airstrike, ice lance, execute and more), control (frost nova, time warp, black hole, gravity grip, force push, earthquake) and summons (call to arms, stone golem, fire sentry). Newest: Perfect Guard (a 2.5s parry), Safe Haven (warp to safety and heal), Blade Flurry, Landmines, Soul Chain (drag a foe to you), War Cry, Overcharge (special and super ready again), Fireball, Smite and Frost Armor. Each has its own cooldown.

ITEMS

Glowing gems appear on the floor. Walk over one to pick it up (you can carry 4), then use it with 1-4 or by tapping it: speed, strength, shield, haste, heal, regeneration, vampire, bomb, frost nova, thorns (attackers take half back), cloak (invisible), lightning jar (zaps 5 foes), turret, monster egg (2 monsters fight for you), frenzy (fast, strong and quick), iron skin (half damage), hourglass (slows every foe), gold rush (double coins) and elixir (full heal and cure).

MINIGAMES

In the EVENTS menu. One is on at a time and a new one comes every 10 minutes. Each has its own traps, hands you its own weapons and armour (yours stay at home, abilities are off), gives you one life and a goal against the clock: kill 30 spitters in a minute, survive the vents, beat three elite knights... Win and you get a random uncommon weapon (one prize per minigame; with all ten, 50,000 coins). Free, as often as you like.

TRAPS

VENTS come three at a time, joined under the floor. Step on one and you drop inside, where nobody can see or hit you. Tap (or click) another vent to travel to it; press anything else to climb out. Every second inside hurts, and more each second, so get out quickly. Also new: tar pits (crawl), bear traps (bite and hold), shock plates (stun), flashbangs (blinding), mortars (shells rain down round them), wind fans (blow everyone away), crushers (a long warning, then a huge hit), oil slicks (you slide) and steam geysers (hurt, throw and burn).

Traps appear around the arena and hurt everyone, monsters included – so lure enemies onto them. Spikes, fire vents and tesla coils glow while they arm, then go off. Others: mines, snares, poison pools, spring pads and warp runes. Watch out too for frost plates (freeze), saw pits, gravity wells (drag you in), lava cracks, root vines (can't move), powder kegs (huge blast), mirror runes (controls reversed), null fields (no specials, supers or abilities) and fault lines – and the kind one, the healing spring.

MONSTERS & BOSSES

Each kind fights its own way: runners are fast, brutes hit hard, spitters and infernals shoot from range, wraiths blink next to you, and armoured ones (grey mark on their health bar) shrug off part of every hit.

Bombers light a fuse next to you and blow up (get out of the ring, or parry) – kill one first and it blows up its own pack. Shamans hang back healing and warding the monsters around them (green glow: they take less damage), so hunt them first. Splitters burst into two quick slimelets. Chargers lower their heads and show a red line, then ram along it – step aside and they hit the wall dazed, or parry them. Necromancers raise skeletons from green marks, and every skeleton crumbles when the necromancer dies.

Light is rare (from wave 6, never two waves running) and usually comes alone. He is blindingly fast: a glinting arc means a lunging blade sweep, after which he blinks somewhere else; a dashed arrow means a chain of four dashes that curve after you. Parry either to stop him and leave him dazed. Beat him three times to earn your way toward the Light Blade.

Reading what hit a monster: ice = a block of ice with icicles · stun = gold stars circling its head · shock = blue lightning arcs · quake = rubble and dust at its feet · time stop = a gold bubble with a stopped clock · roots = green vines · chained = iron chains and a padlock · smite = a halo and a pillar of light · rift = a swirling purple vortex · silenced = a struck-through spark. Slowed monsters are tinted too: cyan with snowflakes (frost), amber with an hourglass (time), rope-brown (snared) or green with bubbles (toxic cloud).

Elites (a gold crown over the head) come in deep waves and in EXTREME. The Elite Sniper beams you, shoots and teleports away. The Elite Samurai throws fans of three slash waves, flashes through you far more often, and SHADOW STEPS: he appears right beside you, then cuts through (parry it!). The Elite Light chains six dashes, bursts a nova of light bolts whenever he blinks away, and calls down JUDGMENT, pillars of light on and around you that stun. The Elite Knight raises a mirror shield that sends your shots back, charges behind it, slams the ground to throw you, and at half health calls two Mirror Knights.

When you die you come back at the safest spot in the arena, away from monsters, traps and shots.

The Giant swings his tree and slams the ground to send out a shockwave – parry either one to stun him. The Portal Mage throws fireballs through red portals, teleports (follow him through the portal if you are quick), lays traps, summons monsters through green portals and, as a last resort, hides behind two Giants – kill them to finish him.

SAVING

Type a password on the start screen and your XP, coins, weapons, upgrades, abilities and skins are saved to it. Use the same password on any device to carry on. A copy is also kept in your browser, so progress survives server updates.
